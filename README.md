# PolicySevak | Multilingual Policy, Rights & Scheme Explainer

> An intelligent system that ingests government notifications, welfare policy drafts, or municipal laws and converts them into plain, regional-language voice explainers highlighting exact rights, eligibility, and real-world impacts for citizens and small businesses.

---

## 🚀 Overview
Navigating complex government policies, legal notifications, and welfare schemes is often hindered by bureaucratic jargon, language barriers, and lack of awareness. **PolicySevak** solves these challenges by providing a 10-in-1 interactive platform that bridges the gap between official policy drafts and everyday citizens.

---

## ✨ Key Features & Solutions Provided

Based on the core problem statements, **PolicySevak** features 10 dedicated pillars:
1. **AI Simplification**: Transforms dense, jargon-heavy official notifications into clear, plain-language summaries.
2. **Regional Translation**: Translates summarized policies instantly into Indian regional languages (Hindi, Bengali, Assamese, Telugu, Tamil).
3. **Smart Scheme Discovery**: Explores central and state welfare schemes categorized by sectors (Agriculture, MSME, Education, Startups).
4. **Smart Eligibility Checker**: Evaluates applicant profile parameters (income, profession, category) against scheme criteria in real-time.
5. **Rights & Duties Explainer**: Outlines legal protections, grievance redressal rights, and civic responsibilities.
6. **Voice Explainer**: Provides audio summaries with animated voice visualizer and player controls.
7. **Step-by-Step Guidance**: Offers a clear, interactive roadmap for document preparation and portal applications.
8. **Source Verification**: Displays official Gazette numbers, publication timestamps, and cryptographic checksums.
9. **AI Policy Chatbot**: An interactive assistant to answer user queries regarding policies and procedures.
10. **Policy Change Tracker**: Real-time amendment alerts and circular notifications affecting citizens and businesses.
